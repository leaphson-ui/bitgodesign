Scenes

The 2D canvas scenes from the Cantor8 homepage (line and grid), the Save homepage (dots) and the homepage ASCII (ascii), as one library. A page places one by name: <canvas data-scene="name" data-scene-opts='{…}'>, then Scenes.mount(root). Files: shared/system/scenes.js, scenes.css.

Each scene is shown on the ink card and the paper card at multicard-card size, and as the 50/50 visual on the blue page. The ink and blue frames use the example options; the paper frame uses the defaults, with no labels. Tone follows the ground: a canvas inside .tone-paper draws navy and blue; anywhere else it draws white and blue. tone: 'light' | 'dark' names the ground and overrides that.
